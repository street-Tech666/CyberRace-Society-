# CyberRace Society

CyberRace Society is a vision for an inclusive, voice-first community connecting people, personal AI agents, and supported digital and physical technology.

## Direction

- Let people use compatible AI agents and connect services and devices with their permission.
- Offer accessible, mobile-friendly experiences and support for different needs and backgrounds.
- Explore voice interaction across supported home, work, communication, and entertainment tools.
- Make privacy, revocable permissions, and human approval for sensitive actions central to the design.

## Status

This repository contains a project overview. The capabilities above are a proposed vision, not a claim of completed integrations or live device controls. Compatibility and security must be tested as the project develops.
